# Description (App Store / Play full description)

mock.edumimi helps Nigerian students prepare for JAMB, WAEC, and NECO with timed full mocks, topic drills, and past papers.

**Practice**
- Personalized dashboard with weak-topic insights
- Exam catalog by type, subject, and mode
- Rich LaTeX and HTML question rendering

**Stay consistent**
- Daily practice streak tracking
- Optional push reminders when your streak is at risk

**Go further**
- Offline practice with automatic sync when you reconnect
- Paystack checkout for premium packs
- Study Squad community chat
- Leaderboard and score history

Part of the Edumimi learning platform. Independent practice platform — not affiliated with JAMB, WAEC, NECO, or any exam board.
